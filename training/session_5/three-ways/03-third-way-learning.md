# The Third Way: Continual learning and experimentation

Pair 3 · Session 5 · "Teach one of the Three Ways in three minutes"

## Step 1. What Claude would say

**The idea.** The Third Way is about culture, the way people work together. It creates a place where people keep trying new things, take risks, and learn from both success and failure. It also says that repeating and practicing is how we become really good. What one person learns becomes knowledge for the team, and then for the whole company.

**How to get there.** See mistakes as a chance to learn, not a reason to punish (a "fair culture"). Share local discoveries so everyone improves. Build strength into daily work, even by causing small failures on purpose. Keep time to improve the way we work. Leaders show that learning is important.

**Example from the Summit Cafe API.**

- After an incident, the team writes a postmortem (a review of what happened) that does not blame anyone, and notes what to change.
- A lesson from one pull request, for example a common mistake in branch names, becomes a rule in the repository guide for everyone.
- A Game Day: the team breaks the café's API on purpose, in a safe place, to see how it recovers.
- A skill that saves time for one person is shared in the repository, so the whole group can use it.

**One sentence to remember.** Learning means: it is safe to try, safe to fail, and what we learn is shared.

## Step 2. Check against the official slides (64 to 76)

**Where to look.** Slides 74 and 75.

**What the slides say, in short.**

- Slide 74 presents the Third Way: a culture of continual experimentation (which needs risk-taking and learning from success and failure) and the idea that repeating and practicing is the base of mastery. It compares the three Ways: the First Way is work moving left to right, the Second Way is fast feedback from right to left, and the Third Way is a culture of continual learning.
- Slide 75 lists the five capabilities of the Third Way. A capability is a skill or ability that a team needs.

**The five capabilities** (the order on the slide may be different):

1. Make the improvement of daily work a habit of the organization
2. Enable learning in the organization and a safe culture
3. Turn local discoveries into improvements for everyone
4. Build strength (resilience) into our daily work
5. Leaders support a learning culture

**Compare with Step 1.**

- Does the explanation say that this Way is about **culture**, and not about a tool?
- Does it connect what one person learns to what the team and the whole company learn?
- Does it mention the **role of leaders**? It is easy to forget.
- Is a safe culture, with no blame, part of the answer? The official material says that a fair response to accidents is needed before people can learn.

**Watch out for.** Chaos engineering and Game Days are examples of building strength, but they are not the whole Third Way. The Way is the culture that makes them possible.
